# JobberTrain Pilot Onboarding

## Pilot objective

Validate that a manufacturer can publish product knowledge, assign it to the right retailer or team members, and see measurable completion without JobberTrain staff guiding every step.

## Before inviting participants

- Confirm the academy logo, colors, landing page, and sign-in page.
- Publish at least one product family with representative variations.
- Publish one short course and one quiz.
- Confirm quiz passing score, attempt limit, course block order, and certificate rules.
- Add or import the participating retailer locations.
- Confirm notification settings. Keep optional emails off unless the brand approves them.
- Assign the pilot training with a realistic due date.
- Test one invitation with a non-admin email before inviting the full group.

## Manufacturer administrator walkthrough

1. Sign in through the branded academy.
2. Open **Learn** to review Quizzes, Courses, Products, My Training, My Academies, and My Certificates.
3. Open **Manage** to review Retailers, Assignments, Certifications, Notifications, Reports, Users, and Brand.
4. Add or import retailer locations.
5. Invite manufacturer team members and retailer learners with the minimum access they need.
6. Assign the pilot course or quiz.
7. Confirm the assignment email and branded destination.
8. Review progress and results from Reports.

## Learner walkthrough

1. Open the invitation email and accept the academy invitation.
2. Create a JobberTrain password, or sign in with an existing JobberTrain account.
3. Confirm the learner lands inside the invited academy.
4. Open **My Training** and begin the assigned course or quiz.
5. Leave once during training, return, and confirm progress is preserved.
6. Complete the training and confirm the result appears.
7. Open **My Certificates** if the completed training qualifies for one.
8. Open **My Academies** and confirm only invited academies appear.

## Pilot support rules

- Use one named manufacturer administrator as the primary contact.
- Record the page, user role, device, and approximate time for every issue.
- Include a screenshot for visual or navigation issues.
- Do not send passwords, API keys, or private customer data in feedback.
- Classify feedback as **Blocker**, **Important**, or **Improvement**.

## Launch readiness checks

- Invitation acceptance works for new and existing JobberTrain accounts.
- Password reset returns to the correct branded academy.
- Manufacturer, retailer, and viewer permissions match their roles.
- A user cannot see another academy unless invited.
- Assignments and learner progress persist across sign-in sessions.
- Course, quiz, product, certificate, and report views work on desktop and mobile.
- Notification emails use the correct academy branding and destination.
- Empty, loading, and error states explain what the user should do next.

## Recommended pilot size

Start with one manufacturer administrator, one manufacturer viewer, two retailer locations, and five to ten learners. This is enough to exercise permissions, assignments, reporting, email delivery, and the learner experience without creating unnecessary cleanup.
